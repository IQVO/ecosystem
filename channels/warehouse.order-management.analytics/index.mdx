## Overview
  The analytics fan-out topic (`kafka.AnalyticsTopic` in the code), separate from the integration topic so the OLTP integration contract and the analytical read-model stream evolve independently (ADR 0006). order-management publishes every analytics-relevant domain event here; cmd/order-projector is the sole consumer and the only writer of the analytical store.
