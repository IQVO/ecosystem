## Overview
  Internal analytics topic (ADR-0010), matching the `AnalyticsTopic`
constant in `internal/adapters/outbound/kafka/analytics_publisher.go`.
Every domain event this context raises is published here as a
CloudEvents 1.0 structured-mode event with dataschema
`urn:warehouse:workforce-management:analytics:<EventName>:v1`. Consumed
by this service's own analytics projector (`workforce-analytics`
consumer group), which dedupes on `id` and dead-letters anything that
fails CloudEvents validation to `warehouse.workforce.analytics.dlq`.
