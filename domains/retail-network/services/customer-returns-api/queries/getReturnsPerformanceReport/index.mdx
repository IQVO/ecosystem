## Overview
Per UTC day of the business instant (`authorizedAt`, `receivedAt`): the returns authorized (a return is born authorized, so this is both "opened" and "approved"), the returns received (`ReturnCompleted`) and, over the receipts whose authorization is also projected, the p50 and p95 of `receivedAt - authorizedAt` in seconds (`null` on a day without a sample; a negative duration counts as 0). `reasonPareto` ranks the range's authorizations by reason code, at most 5 entries; `share` is over ALL authorizations of the range. `freshness` is the projection's lag. Days without activity are omitted, an empty range answers empty arrays. The return RATE and the refund figures are deliberately absent: their facts are not in this context's own events (ADR 0012).




## GET `(/reports/returns-performance)`

### Parameters
- **from** (query): First UTC day of the range, inclusive. Omitted = 29 days before `to` (a 30-day range).
- **to** (query): Last UTC day of the range, inclusive. Omitted = today. At most 366 days from `from`.
