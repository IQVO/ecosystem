## Overview
Returns the offer in any state (QUOTED, EXPIRED or CONSUMED) with its witness path, basis and policy version. Offers are kept for a retention period after they expire.




## GET `(/delivery-offers/{offerId})`

### Parameters
- **offerId** (path) (required): The DeliveryOffer id.
