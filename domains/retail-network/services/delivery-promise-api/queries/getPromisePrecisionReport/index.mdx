## Overview
Per UTC day of PromiseMade (its CloudEvents time) and per tier. windowMinutes is the mean / p50 / p90 of deliverByLatest - deliverByEarliest in minutes; exactDayShare is the share of promises whose window starts and ends on the same LOCAL calendar day in the window's timeZone (ADR 0011); a window without a loadable zone is read in UTC and also counted in zoneUnknown. revisedShare is the share with at least one PromiseRevised; unkeepableCount counts those with a PromiseUnkeepable (both at any time). Days without a promise are omitted. Served by cmd/dp-reports (ADR 0018).




## GET `(/reports/promise-precision)`

### Parameters
- **from** (query): First UTC day of the range, inclusive. Omitted = 29 days before `to` (a 30-day range).
- **to** (query): Last UTC day of the range, inclusive. Omitted = today. At most 366 days from `from`.
