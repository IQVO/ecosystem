## Overview
Per UTC day of judgement (the CloudEvents time of PromiseKept / PromiseBroken), per tier and per basis of the promise AS MADE (UNKNOWN while its PromiseMade is not projected). judged = kept + broken; keptRate = kept / judged; brokenByCause splits broken by the PromiseBroken cause (LATE, LOST, RETURNED); lateByMinutes is the continuous p50 / p90 of lateByMinutes over the broken LATE promises, null when there is none. Days without a judgement are omitted. Served by cmd/dp-reports (ADR 0018).




## GET `(/reports/promise-reliability)`

### Parameters
- **from** (query): First UTC day of the range, inclusive. Omitted = 29 days before `to` (a 30-day range).
- **to** (query): Last UTC day of the range, inclusive. Omitted = today. At most 366 days from `from`.
