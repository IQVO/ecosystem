## Overview
Per UTC day and tier, offersQuoted counts DeliveryOfferQuoted and promisesMade counts PromiseMade of that day (each by its own CloudEvents time); conversion = promisesMade / offersQuoted, null when no offer was quoted that day and tier. Same-day counts, not an offer-to-promise cohort. Days with neither are omitted. Served by cmd/dp-reports (ADR 0018).




## GET `(/reports/quote-conversion)`

### Parameters
- **from** (query): First UTC day of the range, inclusive. Omitted = 29 days before `to` (a 30-day range).
- **to** (query): Last UTC day of the range, inclusive. Omitted = today. At most 366 days from `from`.
