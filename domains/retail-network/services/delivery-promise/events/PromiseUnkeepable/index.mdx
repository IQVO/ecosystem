## Description
`type` = `com.retailnetwork.commerce.delivery-promise.promise.PromiseUnkeepable`; `dataschema` = `urn:retailnetwork:delivery-promise:events:PromiseUnkeepable:v1`; subject and key = the promiseId. Topic: retailnetwork.delivery-promise.events. Raised by RevisePromise when RepromiseOnRisk finds no path even at STANDARD (E9, G10). The promise stays AT_RISK. customer-ordering consumes it and cancels the group; its CustomerOrderCancelled then cancels the promise. At most once per promise (a repeated trigger raises nothing). `reason` repeats the risk reason that started the re-promise.
