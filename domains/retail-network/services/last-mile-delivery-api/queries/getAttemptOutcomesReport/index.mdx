## Overview
Per UTC day and per delivery station (the station of the attempt's route, UNKNOWN while its PackageOutForDelivery is not projected): attempts, deliveredAttempts and failedAttempts are the DeliveryAttempted of the day by outcome (DELIVERED, FAILED); packagesAttempted are the packages whose FIRST attempt fell on the day, packagesWithFirstAttemptSuccess those of them delivered at it; firstAttemptSuccessRate = packagesWithFirstAttemptSuccess / packagesAttempted; meanAttemptsPerPackage = all the attempts of those packages (any day) / packagesAttempted; both null without a first attempt. returnsInitiated counts the PackageReturnInitiated of the day (its CloudEvents time) at the station of the package's latest attempt. Days without any of these are omitted.




## GET `(/reports/attempt-outcomes)`

### Parameters
- **from** (query): First UTC day of the range, inclusive. Omitted = 29 days before `to` (a 30-day range).
- **to** (query): Last UTC day of the range, inclusive. Omitted = today. At most 366 days from `from`.
