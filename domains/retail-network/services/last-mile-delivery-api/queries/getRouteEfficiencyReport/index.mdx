## Overview
Per UTC day of completion and per delivery station (stationNodeId), over the completed routes: a route counts as completed when every package it took out for delivery has an attempt (the precondition of CompleteRoute, which raises no event) and its RoutePlanned is projected; its day is the day of its last attempt. routesCompleted is their count; stopsPerRoute = stops / routesCompleted (stops = the routes' RoutePlanned stopCount); packagesPerStop = packages attempted / distinct stops attempted (every stop of a completed route was attempted, so the denominator is the stops); declaredCostPerStopCents = the stop-weighted mean of the waves' declared costPerStopCents over the routes whose wave declared a cost, null when none did; costPerPackageCents = declaredCostPerStopCents x the stops of those routes / their packages, null when undefined. Days without a completed route are omitted.




## GET `(/reports/route-efficiency)`

### Parameters
- **from** (query): First UTC day of the range, inclusive. Omitted = 29 days before `to` (a 30-day range).
- **to** (query): Last UTC day of the range, inclusive. Omitted = today. At most 366 days from `from`.
