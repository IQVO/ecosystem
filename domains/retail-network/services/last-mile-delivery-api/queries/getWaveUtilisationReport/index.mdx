## Overview
Per serviceDate and delivery station of the scheduled waves: wavesScheduled, wavesCancelled (those with a DeliveryWaveCancelled), stopCapacity (the sum of the scheduled stopCapacity) and stopsInducted (the sum of each wave's highest assignedStops of DeliveryWaveCapacityChanged, 0 without one); utilisation = stopsInducted / stopCapacity, null when the capacity is 0. Cancelled waves stay in every sum. Dates without a wave are omitted.




## GET `(/reports/wave-utilisation)`

### Parameters
- **from** (query): First UTC day of the range, inclusive. Omitted = 29 days before `to` (a 30-day range).
- **to** (query): Last UTC day of the range, inclusive. Omitted = today. At most 366 days from `from`.
