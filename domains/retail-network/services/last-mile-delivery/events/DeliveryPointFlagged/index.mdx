## Description
`type` = `com.retailnetwork.logistics.last-mile-delivery.deliverypointprofile.DeliveryPointFlagged`; subject and key = the stopKey (opaque; no address, name or free text anywhere in the event). Raised by RecordAttempt, in the same transaction as the attempt, while a DeliveryPointProfile holds at least two open strikes (a strike is one failed visit reported as NOT_HOME or ACCESS_PROBLEM; one strike per route; strikes older than 60 days from the newest attempt do not count) and the attempt reached packages that were not told yet. `trackingNumbers` holds only the newly affected packages (1 to 5); `failures` is the number of strikes open at that moment; `reason` is ACCESS_PROBLEM when any open strike has it, else NOT_HOME; `flaggedAt` is the attempt instant that triggered it. No optional fields. No event means no new package was affected; there is no cleared event. Consumers are idempotent per tracking number. Dormant until the driver client sends an outcomeDetail on the attempt (ADR 0014).
