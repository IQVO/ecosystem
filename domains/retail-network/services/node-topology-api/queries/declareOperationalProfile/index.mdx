## Overview
Appends a profile revision; earlier revisions are never edited. Called by vendor-connectivity (declaredBy VENDOR) and by the network-ops planner (declaredBy NETWORK_OPS). Every day of a cutoff must fall inside the operating hours of that day, and a revision of an ACTIVE FULFILLMENT_CENTER keeps at least one cutoff and a handlingTime above zero. sortDwellMinutes is accepted only for SORTATION_CENTER and AIR_HUB nodes. The optional inbound limits (maxCartonWeightG, maxCartonDimsMm, acceptsPallets, requiresSscc) are declared per revision; a revision without inbound declares none, and consumers then treat inbound limits as unchecked. Publishes NodeOperationalProfileDeclared.




## PUT `(/nodes/{nodeId}/operational-profile)`

### Parameters
- **nodeId** (path) (required)
- **Idempotency-Key** (header) (required): Client-chosen key. A repeat on the same route with the same key and request replays the first response. Keys are kept for 24 hours. vendor-connectivity sends its transactionId.
