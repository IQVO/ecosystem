## Description
`type` = `com.retailnetwork.logistics.node-topology.servicearea.ServiceAreaAssigned`; subject and key = the postalPrefix. Raised by AssignServiceArea, on first assignment and on every reassignment. Subject and Kafka key are the postalPrefix. `assignmentVersion` strictly increases per prefix; consumers keep the highest. `regionId` is the station's region.
