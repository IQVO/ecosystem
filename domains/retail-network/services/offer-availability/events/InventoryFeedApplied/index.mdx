## Description
`type` = `com.retailnetwork.commerce.offer-availability.inventoryfeed.InventoryFeedApplied`; subject and key = the feedId. Raised once by ApplyInventoryFeed when the feed reaches APPLIED, after the ListingAvailabilityChanged events of the same transaction. A FAILED or SUPERSEDED feed raises nothing. `lineCount` is the number of lines as received.
