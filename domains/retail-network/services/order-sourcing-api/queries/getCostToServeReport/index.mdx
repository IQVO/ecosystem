## Overview
Over the LAST sourcing decision of each assignment (highest releaseNo), per UTC day of its decidedAt: costCompleteShare = decisions with a complete estimate / decisions; costPerUnitCents = the sum of estimatedCostCents of the complete decisions / the sum of their units (null when undefined); p50CostCentsPerGroup and p90CostCentsPerGroup = the continuous percentiles of estimatedCostCents per group over the complete decisions (null without one). Estimates are planner-declared cents per package-unit (ADR 0011). Days without a decision are omitted.




## GET `(/reports/cost-to-serve)`

### Parameters
- **from** (query): First UTC day of the range, inclusive. Omitted = 29 days before `to` (a 30-day range).
- **to** (query): Last UTC day of the range, inclusive. Omitted = today. At most 366 days from `from`.
