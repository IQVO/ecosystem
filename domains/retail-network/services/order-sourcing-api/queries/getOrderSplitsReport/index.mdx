## Overview
Per UTC day of FulfillmentQueued (an order belongs to the day its first group was queued): ordersQueued = the distinct orders; groupsPerOrder = their queued groups / ordersQueued; splitOrderShare = orders with more than one group / ordersQueued. Days without a queued order are omitted.




## GET `(/reports/order-splits)`

### Parameters
- **from** (query): First UTC day of the range, inclusive. Omitted = 29 days before `to` (a 30-day range).
- **to** (query): Last UTC day of the range, inclusive. Omitted = today. At most 366 days from `from`.
