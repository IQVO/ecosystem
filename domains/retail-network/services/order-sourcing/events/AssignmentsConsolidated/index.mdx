## Description
`type` = `com.retailnetwork.commerce.order-sourcing.assignment.AssignmentsConsolidated`; subject and key = the carrier's assignmentId; stream `analytics` (topic `retailnetwork.order-sourcing.analytics`). Raised by ReleaseToNode in the SAME transaction as the carrier's FulfillmentReleasedToNode (ADR 0015). `members` lists every merged group (carrier first, then ascending assignmentId) with its own `units`; `memberCount` is at least 2 and `units` the sum over the members. Analytics only: published ONLY to retailnetwork.order-sourcing.analytics.
