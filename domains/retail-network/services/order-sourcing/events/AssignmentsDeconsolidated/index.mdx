## Description
`type` = `com.retailnetwork.commerce.order-sourcing.assignment.AssignmentsDeconsolidated`; subject and key = the carrier's assignmentId; stream `analytics` (topic `retailnetwork.order-sourcing.analytics`). Raised by the carrier when its merge is dissolved (ADR 0015). `reason` is PROMISE_REVISED (a revision moved the window of the member `triggerAssignmentId` off the merged delivery; FulfillmentReleaseWithdrawn follows), PURCHASE_ORDER_REJECTED or PURCHASE_ORDER_EXPIRED (the vendor refused the carrier's release; every member excludes the node and is re-searched; `triggerAssignmentId` is omitted). `memberAssignmentIds` lists the merged ids, carrier first. A cancel never dissolves a merge. Analytics only: published ONLY to retailnetwork.order-sourcing.analytics.
