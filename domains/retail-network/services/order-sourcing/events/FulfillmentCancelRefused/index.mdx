## Description
`type` = `com.retailnetwork.commerce.order-sourcing.assignment.FulfillmentCancelRefused`; subject and key = the assignmentId; stream `events` (topic `retailnetwork.order-sourcing.events`). Raised instead of FulfillmentCancelled when the request arrives in LOCKED, SHIPPED or DELIVERED (contracts-delta E11, gap G12), or while the group is a member of a MERGED release that is not yet locked (ADR 0015). The state does not change and nothing is withdrawn. `reason` is CANCEL_AFTER_LOCK, or CANCEL_WHILE_MERGED for the merged case, where the request can be retried after the carrier's lock or after a dissolution. The vendor ships and the shopper path becomes a return. Consumer: customer-ordering.
