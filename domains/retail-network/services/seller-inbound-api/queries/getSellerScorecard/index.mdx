## Overview
The seller's compliance scorecard over a rolling window that ends now, derived from this service's own facts (ADR 0005): the shipments that closed in the window, the
cartons they reported and how many were `RECEIVED`, the discrepancies recorded by kind, and the compliance violations recorded. Facts are dated by the instant this service
recorded them, not by the `receivedAt` the vendor reports.

`receiptAccuracyRate` is `cartonsReceived / cartonsReported` and is omitted when no carton was reported; `discrepancyRatePerShipment` is the discrepancies recorded divided by
`shipmentsReceived` and is omitted when no shipment closed. Rates are rounded to four decimals.

`complianceTier` is the worst of three signals (thresholds are declared in code and in ADR 0005 A3): `AT_RISK` when `receiptAccuracyRate` is below 0.90, `discrepancyRatePerShipment`
is 2.0 or more, or there are 5 or more violations; otherwise `WATCH` when the rate is below 0.98, the discrepancy rate is 0.5 or more, or there is at least 1 violation; otherwise `GOOD`.
The tier is **informational**: it changes nothing about the seller, who stays `ACTIVE` or `SUSPENDED` by the human commands only. A suspended seller has a scorecard too.
The read publishes no event.





## GET `(/sellers/{sellerId}/scorecard)`

### Parameters
- **sellerId** (path) (required): The seller id.
- **window** (query): The rolling window that ends now. Defaults to `30d`. Any other value is `invalid-window`.
