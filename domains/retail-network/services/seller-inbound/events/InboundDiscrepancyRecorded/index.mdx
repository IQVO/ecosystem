## Description
`type` = `com.retailnetwork.partner.seller-inbound.inboundshipment.InboundDiscrepancyRecorded`; subject and key = the aggregate root id; stream `analytics`. Raised per difference (policy RecordDiscrepancies); the manifest is never edited. `kind` is the receiving side's carton discrepancy kind string, passed through: MissingCarton, UnexpectedCarton, Damaged, Short or Over. For Short and Over, `expected` and `actual` are `<gtin>:<qtyEaches>`. Analytics only.
