## Overview
Per UTC day of the outcome instant (deliveredAt, returnedAt, declaredAt): packages delivered, delivered at the first attempt (no DELIVERY_FAILED scan), returned to the sender and lost. firstAttemptSuccessRate = firstAttemptDelivered / (packagesDelivered + packagesReturnedToSender + packagesLost); lostRate and returnToSenderRate share that denominator; meanAttemptsPerDeliveredPackage = (packagesDelivered + DELIVERY_FAILED scans of the delivered packages) / packagesDelivered, null on a day without deliveries. Days without an outcome are omitted.




## GET `(/reports/delivery-outcomes)`

### Parameters
- **from** (query): First UTC day of the range, inclusive. Omitted = 29 days before `to` (a 30-day range).
- **to** (query): Last UTC day of the range, inclusive. Omitted = today. At most 366 days from `from`.
