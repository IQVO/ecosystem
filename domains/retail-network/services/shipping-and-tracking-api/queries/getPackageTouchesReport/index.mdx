## Overview
Per UTC day of deliveredAt and per tier (UNKNOWN while the package's LabelIssued is not projected): the delivered packages and the touches per package (mean, p50, p90 continuous percentiles). A touch is an applied journey scan of type PICKUP_DEPARTURE, DEPARTURE, TRANSIT_ARRIVAL, STATION_ARRIVAL, OUT_FOR_DELIVERY or DELIVERY_FAILED (SHIPMENT_CONFIRMED and DELIVERED are not touches). Days without a delivery are omitted.




## GET `(/reports/package-touches)`

### Parameters
- **from** (query): First UTC day of the range, inclusive. Omitted = 29 days before `to` (a 30-day range).
- **to** (query): Last UTC day of the range, inclusive. Omitted = today. At most 366 days from `from`.
