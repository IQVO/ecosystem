## Overview
One inbound shipment as the pallet-and-carton tree. A shipment that has not shipped
yet, or that is bound for another vendor's node, is `not-found`. Unlike the
poll, this read also answers for a shipment that was already received
(`status` `CLOSED` or `CLOSED_WITH_DISCREPANCY`).





## GET `(/vendor/inbound-shipments/{shipmentId})`

### Parameters
- **vendorId** (query) (required): The calling vendor (G32). Ownership of the addressed resource is checked against it.
- **shipmentId** (path) (required): The seller-inbound shipment id.




### Responses

#### <span className="text-green-500">200 OK</span>
The pallet-and-carton tree of one inbound shipment: shipment > pallet > carton > item.
Cartons that are not on a pallet are top-level; a pallet SSCC is the `parentSscc` of its cartons.
