## Description
`type` = `com.retailnetwork.partner.vendor-connectivity.returnoutcome.VendorReturnOutcomeSubmitted`; the first outcome that fits an open authorization completes it (RECEIVED, `ReturnCompleted`). Lines beyond the authorization (an unexpected return) are recorded on the local outcome facts, flagged, and never completed on. A duplicate `id` is swallowed by the inbox.
