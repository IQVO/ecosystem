## Description
`type` = `com.retailnetwork.storefront.checkout.checkoutsession.CheckoutDeclined`; subject and key = the sessionId. Raised by RecordDecline, and also when the authorized amount is not the quote total (reason `authorization-mismatch`, the authorization is voided). The session stays open for another token while the quote is valid. Analytics only.
