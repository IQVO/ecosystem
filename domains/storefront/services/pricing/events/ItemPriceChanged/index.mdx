## Description
`type` = `com.retailnetwork.storefront.pricing.itemprice.ItemPriceChanged`. Raised on item creation and whenever the effective price moves: SetListPrice, a sale window starting or ending (PublishEffectivePriceOnWindowBoundary), ScheduleSale of an already open window and CancelSale of a live sale (CancelSale has no event of its own). Subject and key = the catalogItemId. `saleEndsAt` is present only while a sale is live.
