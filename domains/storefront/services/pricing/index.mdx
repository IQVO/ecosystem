## Purpose
Owns what a shopper pays: the list price and sale windows per catalogue item, the delivery charge per service tier, a synthetic tax table, and the binding, immutable, expiring PriceQuote a checkout session is confirmed against. One configured currency, integer minor units; promotions are a later phase (D10).

## Contract


Event catalog for the **pricing** bounded context of the storefront (Core subdomain): list prices and sale windows per catalogue item, the delivery charge per service tier, a synthetic tax table and the binding PriceQuote.

**Published.** Five integration events through a **transactional outbox** (the aggregate row and its already-encoded events are written in ONE database transaction; an always-on relay drains them to Kafka, at-least-once). `ItemPriceChanged`, `ItemPriceWithdrawn`, `DeliveryChargeScheduleChanged` and `TaxTableChanged` go to `retailnetwork.pricing.events`; `PriceQuoteIssued` is analytics only and goes to `retailnetwork.pricing.analytics`. `ItemPriceChanged` is raised whenever the effective price moves: a list-price change, a sale window starting or ending (clock-driven policy PublishEffectivePriceOnWindowBoundary) or a CancelSale of a live sale (CancelSale has no event of its own). There is no PriceQuoteExpired event: quote expiry is checkout-internal TTL state.

**Consumed.** `CatalogItemCreated` and `CatalogItemDiscontinued` from offer-availability feed the local CatalogItemView; a discontinued item also runs the WithdrawOnDiscontinue policy (its ACTIVE price is withdrawn and `ItemPriceWithdrawn` published). Only the fields the service relies on are listed; unknown fields are ignored. The consumer group name comes from the mandatory variable OFFER_AVAILABILITY_CONSUMER_GROUP.

**Envelope (mandatory).** CloudEvents 1.0, structured content mode, Kafka header `content-type: application/cloudevents+json; charset=UTF-8`. `specversion`, `id`, `source` (`/retailnetwork/pricing`), `type`, `subject`, `time`, `datacontenttype` (`application/json`) and `dataschema` (`urn:retailnetwork:pricing:events:<EventName>:v1`) are all required. Kafka key = the subject. Type = `com.retailnetwork.storefront.pricing.<entity>.<EventName>`. A breaking payload change is a new `.v2` type. Dispatch on the full `type`, ignore unknown types, dedupe on `id`.

**Ordering.** Item events share the catalogItemId as key and carry a monotonically increasing `version`: a consumer applies a version only above the last one it applied. Schedule and tax-table events carry the published `version`.

**Conventions.** `data` is camelCase; amounts are integer minor units of the single configured `currency`; instants are RFC 3339 UTC; enums are UPPER_SNAKE; optional fields are omitted when absent (never null).
