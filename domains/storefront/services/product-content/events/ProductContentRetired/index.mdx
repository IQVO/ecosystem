## Description
`type` = `com.retailnetwork.storefront.product-content.productcontent.ProductContentRetired`; subject and key = the catalogItemId. Raised by RetireContent (content manager) and by the RetireOnDiscontinue policy for PUBLISHED content. Discarding a DRAFT and retiring a DRAFT on discontinuation raise nothing: nothing was published. `revision` is the content revision at retirement.
