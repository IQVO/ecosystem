## Purpose
Enriches the network's catalogue item with everything a product page needs and the item master does not have: description, bullets, attributes, media and the category tree. The title stays the item master's; media upload is out of scope (URLs to static assets only).

context's own reports and are never consumed by another context.

## Contract


Event catalog for the **product-content** bounded context of the storefront (Generic subdomain): the description, bullets, attributes and media of a catalogue item and the category tree (ADR 0001, ADR 0002).

**Published.** Three integration events, `ProductContentPublished`, `ProductContentRetired` and `CategoryTreePublished`, on `retailnetwork.product-content.events` through a **transactional outbox** (the aggregate row and its already-encoded events are written in ONE database transaction; an always-on relay drains them to Kafka, at-least-once, the CloudEvents `id` is stored with the outbox row). The three types are catalogued in ADR 0004. Nothing is published on `retailnetwork.product-content.analytics`: the model names no analytics-only event for this context. No event carries a title, a price, stock or PII.

**Consumed.** `CatalogItemCreated` and `CatalogItemDiscontinued` from offer-availability (see the `receive` operation and ADR 0003). They feed the local CatalogItemView; a discontinued item also runs the RetireOnDiscontinue policy. The messages list only the fields this service relies on; the producer's own AsyncAPI file is authoritative and unknown fields are ignored. The consumer group name comes from a mandatory environment variable.

**Envelope (mandatory).** CloudEvents 1.0, structured content mode, Kafka header `content-type: application/cloudevents+json; charset=UTF-8`. `specversion`, `id`, `source` (`/retailnetwork/product-content`), `type`, `subject` (the catalogItemId for content events, the categoryTreeId for tree events), `time`, `datacontenttype` (`application/json`) and `dataschema` (`urn:retailnetwork:product-content:events:<EventName>:v1`) are all required. Kafka key = the subject. Type = `com.retailnetwork.storefront.product-content.<entity>.<EventName>`. A breaking payload change is a new `.v2` type, never a mutation. Dispatch on the full `type`, ignore unknown types, dedupe on `id`.

**Ordering.** `ProductContentPublished` and `ProductContentRetired` of one item share a Kafka key (the catalogItemId) and carry a monotonically increasing `revision`: a consumer applies a revision only above the last one it applied. `CategoryTreePublished` carries the tree `version` and the WHOLE tree, so a consumer replaces its tree when the version is above the last one it applied.

**Conventions.** `data` is camelCase; instants are RFC 3339 UTC; enums are UPPER_SNAKE; optional fields are omitted when absent (never null); arrays are always present.
