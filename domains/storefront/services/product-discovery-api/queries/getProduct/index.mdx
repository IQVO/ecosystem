## Overview
GetProduct, the full ProductView of one catalogue item for the product page (SSR renders this in one call; delivery dates are loaded asynchronously afterwards by the BFF).




## GET `(/products/{catalogItemId})`

### Parameters
- **catalogItemId** (path) (required)
- **If-None-Match** (header): The ETag of a cached representation; when it still matches, the service answers 304 Not Modified with no body.
