## Description
`type` = `com.retailnetwork.storefront.shopping-cart.cart.CartAbandoned`; subject and key = the cartId. Raised by the policy AbandonInactive for an OPEN cart with no change for 30 days. The abandoned cart is deleted by the policy PurgeAbandonedCarts 30 days later, which raises no event. Analytics only.
