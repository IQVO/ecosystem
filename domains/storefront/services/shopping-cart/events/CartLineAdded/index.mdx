## Description
`type` = `com.retailnetwork.storefront.shopping-cart.cart.CartLineAdded`; subject and key = the cartId. Raised by AddLine in the same transaction as the outbox row; `quantity` is the MERGED line quantity (adding an item already in the cart merges quantities, there is no duplicate error). Removal, quantity changes, destination, selection and merge raise nothing, by design (decision H1). Analytics only.
