## Purpose
The VDA 5050 master control of the autonomous mobile robots: it accepts transport legs from material-flow-control, assigns each leg to one robot by distance, battery and availability, releases the route as base and horizon while reserving every node and edge for exactly one robot, resolves deadlocks, sends robots to charge and to park, and keeps a registry of every robot's connection, state, errors and factsheet. Every accepted leg ends in exactly one of LegCompleted or LegFailed.

## Contract


Event contract of the **amr-fleet-control** bounded context (WCS tier, Generic, segment `wes`; ADR 0001, ADR 0003).
Seven facts. material-flow-control (WCS plan 04) consumes five: `LegAssigned`, `ContainerPickedUp`, `LegCompleted`
and `LegFailed` for its subsystem-controller interface (WCS context map relationship 4), and `FleetCapacityChanged`
for its capacity view. `RobotStateChanged` and `DeadlockDetected` have no consumer yet: material-flow-control skips
`RobotStateChanged`, and `DeadlockDetected` (like `LegFailed` with reason `ROBOT_BLOCKED`) is designed for
exception-management (WCS plan 05).

**Published** through a transactional outbox to `warehouse.amr-fleet-control.events`, and the same occurrence
(same CloudEvents `id`, `type` and `data`) to `warehouse.amr-fleet-control.analytics` with the `analytics`
`dataschema` segment. Kafka key = `subject`.

**Envelope (mandatory).** CloudEvents 1.0 structured mode, header
`content-type: application/cloudevents+json; charset=UTF-8`,
`type` = `com.warehouse.wes.amr-fleet-control.<entity>.<EventName>`. Dispatch on the full `type`, ignore unknown
types, dedupe on `id`.

**Time.** `time` and every `*_at` field are amr-fleet-control's clock. `robot_timestamp` is the VDA 5050 header
timestamp of the robot state the fact came from (the emulator's simulated time), present only on such facts.

No VDA 5050 order, base, horizon or edge is ever published; node ids appear only in `DeadlockDetected`.
