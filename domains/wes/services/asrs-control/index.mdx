## Purpose
Store and retrieve containers in the automated storage and retrieval system at the throughput the WCS needs, and own where each container sits inside the rack: position assignment, one storage job per aisle at a time, port presentation, reshuffles and PackML equipment state, over a telegram protocol on MQTT to the shuttles and lifts. To the WMS the whole AS/RS is one logical location; no rack position is ever published.

Every message is a CloudEvents 1.0 structured-mode JSON document (`application/cloudevents+json`).
`<ctx>.events` topics carry the integration contract; `<ctx>.analytics` topics carry projector input for the
context's own reports and are never consumed by another context.

## Contract


Event contract of the **asrs-control** bounded context (WCS tier, Generic, segment `wes`; ADR 0001, ADR 0003).
Five facts for material-flow-control's subsystem-controller interface (WCS context map relationship 5) and,
for `StorageJobFailed` with reason `EQUIPMENT_FAULT`, exception-management.

**Published** through a transactional outbox to `warehouse.asrs-control.events`, and the same occurrence
(same CloudEvents `id`, `type` and `data`) to `warehouse.asrs-control.analytics` with the `analytics`
`dataschema` segment. Kafka key = `subject`.

**Envelope (mandatory).** CloudEvents 1.0 structured mode, header
`content-type: application/cloudevents+json; charset=UTF-8`,
`type` = `com.warehouse.wes.asrs-control.<entity>.<EventName>`. Dispatch on the full `type`, ignore unknown
types, dedupe on `id`.

**Time.** `time` and every `*_at` field are asrs-control's clock. `plc_timestamp` is the PLC telegram's
timestamp (the emulator's simulated time), present only on facts that came from a telegram.

No rack position, reshuffle or internal move is ever published (invariant 6).

**PLC link (MQTT).** The second half of this contract is the AS/RS telegram protocol v1 link to the PLC
(emulated by IQVO/equipment-emulator, `docs/telegram-protocol.md`; ADR 0002): channels `asrs/v1/{asrsId}/down`
and `asrs/v1/{asrsId}/up` on the `plc-link` MQTT server, flat JSON (`application/json`, not CloudEvents),
QoS 1, not retained. asrs-control is the protocol's WCS side, so the operations are written from asrs-control's
point of view: `subscribe` = asrs-control publishes (the down topic), `publish` = asrs-control receives (the up
topic). equipment-emulator's `apis/asyncapi.yaml` describes the same two channels from the PLC side. The
telegrams carry rack positions; they never leave the link (invariant 6).
