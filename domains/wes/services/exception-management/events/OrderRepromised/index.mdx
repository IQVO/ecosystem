source `/warehouse/order-management`, dataschema
`urn:warehouse:order-management:events:OrderRepromised:v1`. Subject and

fact of the auto-resolve rule. The rule reverses the work-unit formula
of the open case's source `order_ref` and compares it with
`order_id`; no match is a no-op (the case stays open).
