## Overview
The WorkBatch read side (ADR 0063): the batch, its status (`OPEN`, `COMPLETED`, `ABANDONED`) and its stops in visit order with their cart compartments and member states. A `DETACHED` member left the walk early (its lease expired and the task returned to the pool).





## GET `(/work-batches/{batchId})`

### Parameters
- **batchId** (path) (required): The batch id, as returned by `POST /stations/{stationId}/work-batches`.
