## Description
Raised by the CPT-missed sweep (`POST /tasks/sweep-cpt-misses`, ADR-0025). Re-fires on every sweep pass while the task stays open past its CPT, so consumers must dedupe on their own (order-management's RepromiseOrder is idempotent by design). Published on `warehouse.fulfillment.events`, keyed by task id. Consumed by order-management.
