## Description
`type` = `com.warehouse.wes.material-flow-control.subsystemcapacity.SubsystemCapacityChanged`; subject and key = `<subsystem_kind>/<subsystem_id>`. Published when any field other than `measured_at` changed, never periodically (ADR 0006). A consumer must branch on `subsystem_kind`: `buffer_limit`, `occupied` and `queue_depth` are meaningful only for `STATION` (0 for `AMR_FLEET` and `ASRS`), and the units mean different things per kind, so never sum `units_available` or `open_work` across kinds (`AMR_FLEET` available = idle and assignable robots and above the charge threshold; `ASRS` available = units not HELD, which may be busy).
> **No consumer by design:** pending: wes-work-planning capacity consumer (plan 05b)
