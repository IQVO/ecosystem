## Overview
For the TransferStuckDetected occurrences in [from, to), `days`: per UTC day and `state`, the
detections (the health ticker re-detects a transfer on every pass while it stays stuck) and the
distinct transfers; `latest`: the newest occurrences, most recent first (ties by transfer id),
at most `limit`. `from` / `to` as in /reports/transfer-funnel. 200 with empty arrays when
nothing matches.





## GET `(/reports/stuck-transfers)`

### Parameters
- **from** (query): RFC 3339; inclusive. Defaults to `to` minus 30 days.
- **to** (query): RFC 3339; exclusive. Defaults to now.
- **limit** (query): How many latest occurrences to return, 1 to 100 (default 20).
