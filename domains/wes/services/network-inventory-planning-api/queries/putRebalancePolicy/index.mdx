## Overview
Upserts the Policy in the body, keyed by its Site + SKU. Idempotent:
a PUT carrying exactly the stored policy writes nothing and answers
200 with outcome UNCHANGED. A changed policy bumps the revision
(outcome UPDATED); a new one is created at revision 1 (201,
outcome CREATED). Optimistic concurrency: send the entry's ETag in
If-Match to update only that revision (412 when it moved on);
without If-Match the PUT applies to the current revision. Two
writers racing on the same revision: one wins, the other gets 409.
422 for an entry refused on its face (missing key, negative
quantity, TargetStock below SafetyStock).





## PUT `(/v1/rebalance-policies)`

### Parameters
- **If-Match** (header): Optimistic concurrency for a catalogue PUT (ADR 0015): the entry's
current revision, as its ETag ("3") or bare (3). The PUT applies only
while the stored revision still equals it (412 version-mismatch
otherwise, including when the entry does not exist). Omit it to
apply the PUT to whatever revision is current.
