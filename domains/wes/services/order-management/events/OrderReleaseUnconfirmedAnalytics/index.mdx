## Description
ADR 0043. Raised ONCE per line, by the housekeeping sweep, when a PENDING release confirmation is older than `RELEASE_CONFIRMATION_TIMEOUT` (default 15m) without a WorkUnitCreated naming it. A FACT, never an action: nothing is cancelled, re-released or retried because of it, and it is not retracted if a late confirmation arrives. Analytics-only — the integration topic `warehouse.order-management.events` keeps its three-event contract — and the in-repo projector ignores the type. `subject` and the Kafka key are the order id; `time` is when the line was flagged, `released_at` when it was released.
