## Overview
Returns the hint with its ranked candidates and its outcome: `Active`
until a claim of one of its candidates by the hinted station marks it
`Consumed`, the clock marks it `Expired`, or a newer completion at the
same station marks it `Superseded`.





## GET `(/hints/{hintId})`

### Parameters
- **hintId** (path) (required): The hint id (`hint-` followed by a unique suffix, at most 64 characters).
