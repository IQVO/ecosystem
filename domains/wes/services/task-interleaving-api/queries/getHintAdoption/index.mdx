## Overview
Do operators act on interleaving hints? `days[]` has one row per UTC
calendar day intersecting `[from, to)` (zeros included); a day's counts
cover the part of the day inside the range. `hints_issued`,
`hints_consumed` and `hints_expired` count the hints whose own milestone
(the `InterleavingHintIssued`, `InterleavingHintConsumed` and
`InterleavingHintExpired` event `time`) fell in the window.
`adoption_rate` is consumed / (consumed + expired) of the hints that
ended in the window, null while none did; a hint superseded by a newer
one emits neither ending event and is in no denominator.
`consumed_first_rank` counts the consumed hints whose candidate was
rank 1 and `mean_detour_m` is the mean detour of the consumed
candidates (null when none). `time_to_consume` is the issued-to-consumed
time of the hints consumed in the window whose issue the projection
also saw; `p50_seconds` / `p95_seconds` are linear-interpolated
percentiles and are null when `count` is 0. `siteId` narrows every
counter to one site (omitted = every site); `granularity` is `day` (the
only bucket; the default). The data is as fresh as
`GET /reports/freshness` says.





## GET `(/reports/hint-adoption)`

### Parameters
- **from** (query): RFC 3339 instant, inclusive. Defaults to `to` minus 30 days.
- **to** (query): RFC 3339 instant, exclusive. Defaults to now. At most 366 days after `from`.
- **siteId** (query): Narrows every counter to one site. Omitted = every site.
- **granularity** (query): The time-bucket resolution; only `day` exists (the default).
