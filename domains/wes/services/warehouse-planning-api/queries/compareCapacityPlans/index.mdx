## Overview
docs/adr/0024 decision 4. ids names 2 to 5 distinct stored plans, all of one site_id and one window (same window_start and window_end); their paths, locations and statuses may differ. Per plan, in the order of ids, it returns the scenario, status, assigned demand and its source, path capacity, capacity over the window, shortage, bottleneck step and constraint and the ADR 0022 labour requirement exactly as stored when the plan was created: nothing is recomputed, so a comparison is reproducible. Read-only; REST only (no MCP tool).





## GET `(/capacity-plans/comparisons)`

### Parameters
- **ids** (query) (required): 2 to 5 distinct plan ids, comma-separated.
