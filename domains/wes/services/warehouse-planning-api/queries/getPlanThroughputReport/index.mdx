## Overview
`days`: per site and UTC day, the plans created (by the CloudEvents time of CapacityPlanCreated) and the plans published (by CapacityPlanPublished) in [from, to) (from inclusive, to exclusive). `latency`: per site, over the plans published in the range whose creation was also observed, the median and 95th percentile (continuous, linear interpolation) of published - created, in seconds. Ordered by day, then site. `from` / `to` as in /reports/bottleneck-frequency. 200 with empty arrays when nothing matches.





## GET `(/reports/plan-throughput)`

### Parameters
- **from** (query): RFC 3339; inclusive. Defaults to `to` minus 30 days.
- **to** (query): RFC 3339; exclusive. Defaults to now.
