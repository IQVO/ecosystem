## Overview
For the plans PUBLISHED in [from, to) (from inclusive, to exclusive), per site and UTC day of the publication: how many were published (ALL of them, with or without shortage, so the rate is meaningful), how many had a shortage (> 0), the total shortage in orders, and `shortage_rate` (plans_with_shortage / plans_published). Days without a published plan are omitted. Ordered by day, then site. `from` / `to` as in /reports/bottleneck-frequency. 200 with `days: []` when nothing matches.





## GET `(/reports/shortage-trend)`

### Parameters
- **from** (query): RFC 3339; inclusive. Defaults to `to` minus 30 days.
- **to** (query): RFC 3339; exclusive. Defaults to now.
