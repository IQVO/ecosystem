## Description
Raised when a plan is published while another PUBLISHED plan exists for
the same `(site_id, location, path_id, window_start, window_end)` (ADR
0024 decision 3). `subject`, the Kafka key and `data.plan_id` are the
OLDER plan, the one superseded; `superseded_by` is the newer plan.
Queued in the same transaction as, and BEFORE, the newer plan's
`CapacityPlanPublished` (then `CapacityShortageDetected` and
`BottleneckDetected` when it is short). SUPERSEDED is terminal: a plan
is superseded at most once and never published again. It carries the
older plan's identity and no quantities. Plans migration 0017 marked
SUPERSEDED when it backfilled a database holding several PUBLISHED
plans of one key raised NO event.
`type` = `com.warehouse.wes.warehouse-planning.capacityplan.CapacityPlanSuperseded`.
