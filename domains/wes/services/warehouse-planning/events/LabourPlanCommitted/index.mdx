## Description
Raised by the `LabourPlan` aggregate on `PUT /labour-plans/{site_id}/{shift_id}/{path_id}`
when the commit created or changed the plan (a PUT that changes nothing raises nothing
and keeps the version), queued in the transactional outbox in the same transaction as
the plan. Carries every field of the plan. `planned_heads` is the caller's
(`heads_source` `request`) or derived from the stored workload forecast as
`ceil(units due in [shift_start, shift_start + hours) / (rate_units_per_hour x hours))`
(`heads_source` `forecast`). `shift_start` is OMITTED (not null) when the plan has none.
workforce-management keeps a copy (its ADR 0043) and applies a message only when its
`version` is greater than the stored one. `type` =
`com.warehouse.wes.warehouse-planning.labourplan.LabourPlanCommitted`; `subject` and
Kafka key = `<site_id>/<shift_id>/<path_id>` (ADR 0023 decision 2).
