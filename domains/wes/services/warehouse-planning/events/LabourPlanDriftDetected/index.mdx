## Description
Raised when the committed heads (workforce-management's `ShiftPlanCommitted`) differ
from the labour plan's `planned_heads` for the same `(site_id, shift_id, path_id)` and
the drift is new: non-zero and different from the last drift recorded for that key. It
never corrects either side. `drift_heads` = `committed_heads - planned_heads` (positive
when workforce committed more heads than the plan requires). `type` =
`com.warehouse.wes.warehouse-planning.labourplan.LabourPlanDriftDetected`; `subject` and
Kafka key = `<site_id>/<shift_id>/<path_id>` (ADR 0023 decision 3).

> **No consumer by design:** pending: new requirement fact (ADR 0023 decision 3); surfaced to planners, no consumer yet
