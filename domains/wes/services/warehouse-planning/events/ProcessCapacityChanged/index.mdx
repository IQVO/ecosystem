## Description
Raised by `ProcessCapacity.AddConstraint` when a registration (new or
re-registered) changes the effective rate: the minimum rate value,
the binding constraint, or both. A registration that leaves the
effective rate untouched raises nothing. `type` =
`com.warehouse.wes.warehouse-planning.processcapacity.ProcessCapacityChanged`.

> **No consumer by design:** pending: new capacity fact (WPL #70); consumer not built yet
