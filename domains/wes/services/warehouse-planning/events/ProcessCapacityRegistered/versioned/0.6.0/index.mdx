## Description
Raised by `ProcessCapacity.AddConstraint` when the FIRST constraint of
(processType, location, window) is registered -- the one that
establishes the native unit; its rate is the effective rate at that
moment. Raised on `POST /process-capacities` and by the labor-capacity
consumer, queued in the transactional outbox in the same transaction
as the aggregate. `type` =
`com.warehouse.wes.warehouse-planning.processcapacity.ProcessCapacityRegistered`;
`subject` and Kafka key =
`<processType>/<location>/<windowStart>/<windowEnd>`.

> **No consumer by design:** pending: new capacity fact (WPL #70); consumer not built yet
