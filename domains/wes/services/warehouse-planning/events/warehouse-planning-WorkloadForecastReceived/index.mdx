## Description
Raised by the `WorkloadForecast` aggregate on `PUT /workload-forecasts/{site_id}/{path_id}`
when the receipt changed the buckets (a receipt equal to the stored one raises nothing),
and queued in the transactional outbox in the same transaction as the forecast. It
carries the totals, not the buckets. `type` =
`com.warehouse.wes.warehouse-planning.workloadforecast.WorkloadForecastReceived`;
`subject` and Kafka key = `<site_id>/<path_id>`. A superset of wes-work-planning's
retired `ChargeForecastReceived` (ADR 0023 decision 1).

> **No consumer by design:** pending: new requirement fact (ADR 0023); no context consumes the forecast yet
