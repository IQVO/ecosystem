## Description
Raised by the WorkUnit aggregate when `EnqueueWorkUnit` admits a new releasable unit of work (carrying its CPT) into the process path's work pool. The unit is queued, not yet released. Actively published to `warehouse.work-planning.events`; the published `data` carries `path_id` and `work_unit_id`.
