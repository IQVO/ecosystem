## Description
Raised by the `AssociateShift` aggregate when an associate's logged
break ends. The associate becomes eligible for a new
`LaborAssignment` again, subject to the certification gate.

**Not on the integration topic.** Published only to the internal
analytics topic `warehouse.workforce.analytics` (dataschema
`urn:warehouse:workforce-management:analytics:<EventName>:v1`).
