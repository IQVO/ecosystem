## Description
Raised by the `AssociateShift` aggregate when an associate begins a
logged break. While a break is active the associate cannot be given a
new `LaborAssignment` — `AssignLabor` rejects the attempt.

**Not on the integration topic.** Published only to the internal
analytics topic `warehouse.workforce.analytics` (dataschema
`urn:warehouse:workforce-management:analytics:<EventName>:v1`).
