## Description
Raised by the `AssociateShift` aggregate when a named qualification
(for example `pack`, `pick` or `hazmat`) is added to an associate.
Certifications gate assignment: `AssignLabor` rejects an associate
who does not hold the path's required certification.

**Not on the integration topic.** Published only to the internal
analytics topic `warehouse.workforce.analytics` (dataschema
`urn:warehouse:workforce-management:analytics:<EventName>:v1`).
