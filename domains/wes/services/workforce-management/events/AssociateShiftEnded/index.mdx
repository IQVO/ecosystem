## Description
Raised by the `AssociateShift` aggregate when an associate's shift
closes. Closing a shift ends every active `LaborAssignment` for that
associate, so downstream consumers should treat this as a terminal
signal for the associate's labor picture on the shift.

**Not on the integration topic.** Published only to the internal
analytics topic `warehouse.workforce.analytics` (dataschema
`urn:warehouse:workforce-management:analytics:<EventName>:v1`).
