## Description
Raised by the `LaborAssignment` aggregate when an associate is
assigned to a process path. The domain enforces two hard invariants
before this event exists: the associate holds the path's required
certification, and the associate has no other ACTIVE assignment (no
double-booking across paths).

Note the path boundary: this places an associate on a *path*, never
on a specific task. Dispatching individual tasks belongs to
Fulfillment Execution.

**Not on the integration topic.** Published only to the internal
analytics topic `warehouse.workforce.analytics` (dataschema
`urn:warehouse:workforce-management:analytics:<EventName>:v1`).
