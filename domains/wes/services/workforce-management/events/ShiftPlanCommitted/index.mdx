## Description
CONSUMED from `warehouse.workforce.events` (producer: workforce-management;
one message per PathPlan line). Becomes a LABOR `CapacityConstraint` on the
ProcessCapacity of ProcessType = upper-case(`path_id`), location =
`building_id`: rate = `planned_heads * planned_rate` (UNIT/HOUR), window =
`[time, time + planned_hours)`. Deduped on the CloudEvents `id`.
