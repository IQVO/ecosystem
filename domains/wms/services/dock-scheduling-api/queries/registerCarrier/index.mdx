## Overview
Puts the carrier with this SCAC in the registry (RegisterCarrier). Idempotent by SCAC: a new SCAC is created `ACTIVE` and answered `201`
with a `Location`; a SCAC that is already registered with exactly this data is a no-op answered `200` (the `mc` is compared in its
normalised form, so `654321` and `MC-654321` are the same); a SCAC registered with DIFFERENT data is refused with 409
`carrier-already-registered`: change a registered carrier with `PATCH` instead. Idempotent by method, no `Idempotency-Key` needed.
Publishes `CarrierRegistered` (not on a no-op).





## PUT `(/carriers/{scac})`

### Parameters
- **scac** (path) (required): The carrier's SCAC, 2..4 upper-case letters (anything else is 422 `invalid-scac`).
