## Description
`type` = `com.warehouse.wms.dock-scheduling.carrier.CarrierRegistered`; subject and Kafka key = SCAC. Analytics twin (same id and type, dataschema `analytics`). Consumed by yard-management (the carrier registry copy, ADR 0016 / YF-1). Full carrier snapshot after the first registration and after each update; `version` is the carrier version.
