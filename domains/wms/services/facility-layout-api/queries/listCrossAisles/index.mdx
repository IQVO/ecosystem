## Overview
Returns every declared CrossAisle (ADR-0017) in the zone — its zone-scoped connections between two of its aisles at a bay ordinal — ordered by from-aisle, to-aisle, bay. A read model: it reflects what was registered, including inactive (decommissioned) connections, so a consumer can discover the declared connections without deriving them from the travel graph's edges.





## GET `(/zones/{zoneId}/cross-aisles)`

### Parameters
- **zoneId** (path) (required): The zone's id, `SITE-AREA-ZONE` — exactly the first three segments of every LocationCode inside it.
