## Description
Raised by `DeclareLocationHandlingUnit` (ADR-0040), i.e. `PUT /locations/{locationCode}/handling-unit`. The slot is identified by `locationCode`, exactly as in `LocationSlotRegistered`. CloudEvents `subject` and Kafka key: `locationCode` (same partition as that location's `LocationSlotRegistered`). **Consumed live** by inventory-storage's facility cache, which uses the handling unit to decide whether a sealed case may be stowed in the bin. Last write wins per `locationCode` (same partition, so ordered).


> **No consumer by design:** pending: consumed live by inventory-storage's facility cache (ADR-0040); its asyncapi must declare the channel+type
