## Overview
Returns the DATA of a license plate label (symbology, human readable
text, SKU and units); the service has no printer driver, the floor
client renders and prints it. A carton the receipt has not accepted
(or never heard of) is 404 `carton-not-found`.





## GET `(/receipts/{receiptId}/cartons/{lpn}/label)`

### Parameters
- **receiptId** (path) (required): The receipt id, `rcpt-` followed by a lowercase UUID.
- **lpn** (path) (required): The carton's license plate - its SSCC, or the internal `LPN-<ulid>` of a carton received without one.
