## Overview
Returns the receipt with its lines, state, version (also as the `ETag`)
and the discrepancies as they stand now (final once the receipt is
`Closed`).





## GET `(/receipts/{receiptId})`

### Parameters
- **receiptId** (path) (required): The receipt id, `rcpt-` followed by a lowercase UUID.
