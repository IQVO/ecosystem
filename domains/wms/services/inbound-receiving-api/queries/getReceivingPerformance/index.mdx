## Overview
How is the dock performing? `days[]` has one row per UTC calendar day
intersecting `[from, to)` (zeros included); a day's counts cover the
part of the day inside the range. `receipts_closed`, `discrepancies`
and `receipt_cycle` follow the receipt's `ReceiptClosed` time;
`lines_received` counts the receiving actions (`ReceiptLineReceived`)
and `units_good` / `units_damaged` sum their quantities by condition.
`receipt_cycle` (open to close) and `dock_dwell` (check-in to
completed) include only receipts and appointments whose opening or
check-in the projection also saw; their `p50_seconds` / `p95_seconds`
are linear-interpolated percentiles and are null when `count` is 0.
`appointments` counts each milestone that fell in the window.

`current` is the state right now, independent of the range:
`open_receipts` (opened, not yet closed), `expected_today` (ASNs not
cancelled whose expected arrival falls in today's UTC day) and
`expected_today_started` (those with a receipt already opened). The
data is as fresh as `GET /reports/freshness` says.





## GET `(/reports/receiving-performance)`

### Parameters
- **from** (query): RFC 3339 instant, inclusive. Defaults to `to` minus 30 days.
- **to** (query): RFC 3339 instant, exclusive. Defaults to now. At most 366 days after `from`.
