## Description
`type` = `com.warehouse.wms.inbound-receiving.asn.AsnLateForArrival`; subject and key = the ASN number.
Raised once per ASN, at `OpenReceipt` and only there (the only place an ASN and an
appointment meet), when `asn_registered_at > arrived_at` of the appointment's earliest
yard visit (ADR 0025); `lateness_seconds` = `asn_registered_at - arrived_at`, whole
seconds, >= 1 by construction. An on-time ASN, a walk-in receipt, an appointment with
no arrival seen yet and a legacy ASN with an unknown registration raise nothing.

> **No consumer by design:** read only by this service's own analytics projector (the asn_timeliness KPI, ADR 0025); no other consumer built yet
