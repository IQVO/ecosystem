## Description
`type` = `com.warehouse.wms.dock-scheduling.dockappointment.DockAppointmentDoorReassigned`,
source `/warehouse/dock-scheduling`; key and subject = `appointment_id`.

`from_door_code` is logged, not checked. dock-scheduling is moving this
type onto its integration topic; until then it never arrives and the
consumer does not depend on it. Receipts already open keep their door.
