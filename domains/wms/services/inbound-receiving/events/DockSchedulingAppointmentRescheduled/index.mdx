## Description
`type` = `com.warehouse.wms.dock-scheduling.dockappointment.DockAppointmentRescheduled`,
source `/warehouse/dock-scheduling`; key and subject = `appointment_id`.
`data` is snake_case. The copy takes `door_code`, `window_start` and
`window_end` at `version` (ADR 0020); `previous_door_code` is logged,
and `site_id`, `previous_window_*` and `arrival_tolerance_minutes` are
ignored. Receipts already open keep their door.
