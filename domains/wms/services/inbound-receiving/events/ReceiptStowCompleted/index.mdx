## Description
`type` = `com.warehouse.wms.inbound-receiving.receipt.ReceiptStowCompleted`;
subject = the receipt id, key = the ASN number. Raised exactly once,
for a Closed receipt opened from an appointment, when every Good
carton has a stow from inventory-storage and the gate arrival from
yard-management is known, whatever order those facts and the close
arrive in. `dock_to_stock_seconds` is `stowed_at - arrived_at` in
whole seconds, never negative; `stowed_at` is the latest stow of a
Good carton and `carton_count` how many Good cartons that is. `At`
(the CloudEvents `time`) is when the completion was observed.

> **No consumer by design:** read by this service's own analytics report; no other consumer built yet
