## Description
`type` = `com.warehouse.wms.inbound-receiving.receipt.ReceivingAdviceIssued`;
subject = the receipt id, key = the ASN number. The domain fact only: this
service renders no EANCOM, X12 or transport. Raised exactly once per
receipt, in the same transaction as `ReceiptClosed` (right after it), for a
receipt closed after this event existed; a receipt closed earlier gets no
advice. `despatch_advice_ref` is the ASN number (the reference to the
despatch advice), `supplier_ref` the supplier as the ASN knows it and
`owner_ref` the 3PL depositor (omitted for fleet-owned stock). `last_carton_at`
is when the last unit was received (the receipt's opening when nothing
was); `within_sla` is `issued_at - last_carton_at <= sla_seconds`
(`RECEIVING_ADVICE_SLA`, default 24 h). Per line the three quantities are
exclusive: `accepted_qty` is the Good units not held, `rejected_qty` the
Damaged ones and `held_qty` the Good units declared held; `received_qty`
is their sum. `discrepancies` carry the line kinds `Short`, `Over`, `Damaged`,
`IncorrectProduct` and `Hold` with `w13_code` and `cbv_disposition`.
The payload is bounded because a message the broker refuses blocks the
outbox (ADR 0007): at most 1000 lines (lines with exceptions first, then
in line order; `lines_total` is the real count and `lines_truncated` is
true when some are left out), at most 1000 SSCCs per line (`sscc_count` is
the real count, `ssccs_truncated` is true when listed ones are fewer) and
at most 200 carton findings (`carton_findings_total`). A consumer that
needs the full list reads the receipt over REST.

> **No consumer by design:** none yet: partner-connectivity renders it as EANCOM RECADV / X12 861 / 944 later (D-3 not taken)
