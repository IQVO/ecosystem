## Overview
The container (sealed case, ADR 0045) with this license plate: SKU, case GTIN, units per pack, quantity in eaches, owner, site and bin (empty until it is stowed), state (SEALED, OPEN or EMPTY), lot, expiry and the receipt line it came from. Side-effect-free.




## GET `(/containers/{lpn})`

### Parameters
- **lpn** (path) (required): The container's license plate (the carton SSCC, or an internal `LPN-<site>-<id>`).




### Responses

#### <span className="text-green-500">200 OK</span>
A sealed case with an identity (ADR 0045). The ledger stays in eaches: `qtyEaches` is the case's content, held by the 1:1 SEALED_CASE stock unit `stockUnitId` once stowed.
