## Overview
A held unit with its receipt line, owner, carton marks and, once decided, its disposition. A unit that was released, returned or scrapped stays readable here as the audit trail. Side-effect-free.




## GET `(/held-stock/{id})`

### Parameters
- **id** (path) (required): The held unit's id (`hu-<uuid>`).




### Responses

#### <span className="text-green-500">200 OK</span>
A quantity held out of usable stock (ADR 0052). It has no bin and no usable quantity, and can never be reserved.
