## Overview
The tote (ADR 0054) with this license plate: its site and bin (its logical location; absent until its first stow) and its contents, one entry per stock unit in the tote, ordered by SKU then stock unit id (empty, never null, for an empty tote). Side-effect-free.




## GET `(/totes/{lpn})`

### Parameters
- **lpn** (path) (required): The tote's license plate: its permanent label, registered once (ADR 0054). One namespace with sealed cases.




### Responses

#### <span className="text-green-500">200 OK</span>
A reusable license-plated tote (ADR 0054) and its contents. Its logical location is the bin it is stowed in.
