## Overview
The sealed or open containers stowed in the bin (ADR 0045), ordered by LPN. Empty containers have no bin and are not listed. Side-effect-free.




## GET `(/bins/{binId}/containers)`

### Parameters
- **binId** (path) (required): The bin to inspect.
