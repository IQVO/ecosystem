## Overview
Commission a reusable tote (ADR 0054): its license plate is its permanent label, printed once (pre-printed by the tote supplier, or by the floor client from the LPN as CODE128); this service mints no LPN and prints no label. Idempotent by method: the first call creates the tote in no bin and answers 201, a repeat answers 200 with the tote as it is now. One license-plate namespace with the sealed cases (ADR 0045): a plate that already names a sealed case is 409 `license-plate-taken`. Raises `ToteRegistered` when the tote is created.




## PUT `(/totes/{lpn})`

### Parameters
- **lpn** (path) (required): The tote's license plate: its permanent label, registered once (ADR 0054). One namespace with sealed cases.
