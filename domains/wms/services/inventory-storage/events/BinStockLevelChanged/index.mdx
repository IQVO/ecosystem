## Description
Raised from the SAME transaction as every ledger change that moves the level of a SKU in a bin (ADR 0040), through the transactional outbox: stow and transfer stow (`STOW`), a confirmed pick (`PICK`), a relocation or RELOCATE task (`RELOCATE`: two events, the source bin then the destination bin), a cycle-count shortfall (`COUNT`), a reservation or transfer allocation (`RESERVE`), and a revoked or expired reservation (`REVOKE`). One event per distinct (bin, sku) per transaction, carrying the FINAL level after the whole transaction: an order whose lines pick the same (bin, sku) raises it once. Nothing is raised when the level did not change (idempotent replays, rejected commands).

`on_hand` and `reserved` are ABSOLUTE levels after the change, never deltas. `on_hand` is the quantity physically in the bin as the ledger sees it (located, still-present units: units a cycle count marked unlocated, and fully picked-out units, are not counted); `reserved` is the part of it bound to active reservations, so `on_hand - reserved` is what a new reservation could still draw from the bin. `version` is the persisted per-(bin, sku) counter, strictly increasing and gapless across restarts and concurrent changes: a consumer keeps the highest version it has seen for a (bin, sku) and ignores any lower one.

`reason` is `STOW`, `PICK`, `RELOCATE`, `REPLENISHMENT`, `COUNT`, `RESERVE`, `REVOKE` or `UNLOCATED`; consumers ignore values they do not know. `REPLENISHMENT` is reserved for the relocation reason the later pick-face replenishment change adds, and `UNLOCATED` for an unlocated receive with a bin: neither is emitted today (a receive names no bin). A bin whose site cannot be determined (not registered, or neither a recorded site custody nor a facility-layout location code) raises no event.

**Integration topic only** (no analytics variant). Kafka key and CloudEvents `subject` are `<bin>:<sku>`, so every level of one pair lands on one partition in version order (ADR 0021). pick-face-replenishment consumes it to detect a forward pick slot below its minimum.
