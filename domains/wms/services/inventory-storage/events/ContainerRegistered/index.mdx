## Description
Raised through the transactional outbox when the Container is created (ADR 0045, 0046): a sealed, single-SKU, Good carton of `ReceiptCartonReceived` whose receipt line is booked for at least the container quantities so far AND whose GTIN is in the local PackagingView. Either arrival order of the line and the carton gives the same result, at most once per LPN. No quantity is booked by it (`StockReceived` of `ReceiptLineReceived` already did, once). Kafka key and `subject` are the LPN. Integration topic only.
> **No consumer by design:** pending: container fact (ADR 0045); no consumer declared in any fleet spec yet
