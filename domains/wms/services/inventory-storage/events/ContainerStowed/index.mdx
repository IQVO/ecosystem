## Description
Raised by StowContainer (ADR 0045) on the first stow and on every move to another bin of the same site. `site_id` is derived from the bin here (the carton event carries no site, contract R6). The SEALED_CASE stock unit is created at the first stow and its eaches enter the bin occupancy; `ItemStowed` and `BinStockLevelChanged` accompany it as for any stow. Kafka key and `subject` are the LPN.
> **No consumer by design:** pending: container fact (ADR 0045); no consumer declared in any fleet spec yet
