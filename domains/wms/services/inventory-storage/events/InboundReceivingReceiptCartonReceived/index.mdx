## Description
CONSUMED, not produced (ADR 0045, 0046; contract section 2 with R6/R7 applied): type `com.warehouse.wms.inbound-receiving.receipt.ReceiptCartonReceived`, `source=/warehouse/inbound-receiving`. `data` is snake_case: `{receipt_id, asn_number, sscc, parent_sscc, sealed, lpn, owner_ref, contents[{line_no, sku, gtin, qty, lot, expiry, condition}], received_at}`. It carries NO site (R6): the container's site is derived at StowContainer from the bin. Unknown fields are ignored.

Only a sealed, single-content, `Good` carton whose `qty` equals the case's units per pack in the PackagingView gets a Container (mixed, unsealed and damaged cartons keep their eaches as ordinary staged stock and get none). The carton is recorded keyed by (`receipt_id`, `line_no`, `lpn`) and settles idempotently in either arrival order relative to `ReceiptLineReceived`: the container exists only when the sum of container quantities of the line stays within the eaches `ReceiptLineReceived` booked, and when `gtin` is known to the PackagingView (otherwise it waits in the pending table). This message books NO quantity. The CloudEvents `id` is claimed in `processed_events` (consumer `inbound-carton`) in the same transaction.
