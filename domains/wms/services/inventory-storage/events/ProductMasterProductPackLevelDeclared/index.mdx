## Description
CONSUMED, not produced (ADR 0046, contract R8): type `com.warehouse.wms.product-master.product.ProductPackLevelDeclared`, `source=/warehouse/product-master`, subject and Kafka key = SKU. Projected into the local PackagingView, keyed by GTIN and by (sku, level); an event applies only when its product `version` is not older than the stored one, and the CloudEvents `id` is claimed in `processed_events` (consumer `product-master-pack-level`). A newly known GTIN settles the cartons that were waiting for it. Unknown fields are ignored.
