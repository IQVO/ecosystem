## Description
Raised by every disposition decision (ADR 0052): RELEASE, RETURN_TO_VENDOR, SCRAP or REWORK. `reason` is the hold reason after the decision (a REWORK sets a new one). Kafka key and `subject` are the held unit id.
> **No consumer by design:** pending: held-stock fact (ADR 0052); no consumer declared in any fleet spec yet
