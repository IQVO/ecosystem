## Description
Raised by a RELEASE disposition (ADR 0052). The quantity re-enters usable stock through the staged-receipt path, so a `StockReceived` follows in the same transaction; it is usable once stowed. Kafka key and `subject` are the held unit id.
> **No consumer by design:** pending: held-stock fact (ADR 0052); no consumer declared in any fleet spec yet
