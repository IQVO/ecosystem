## Description
Raised by the ReleaseTransferAllocation use case (ADR 0051) when a TransferAllocationReleaseRequested command was decided — the compensation leg of the transfer saga, so a stuck or abandoned ALLOCATED transfer never strands its origin reservation until lazy expiry. `outcome` is a CLOSED set: RELEASED (the reservation still held the stock; this revoked it and returned the quantity to usable), ALREADY_RESOLVED (the reservation had already expired or been confirmed — or the line's allocation was REJECTED, so no hold ever existed; either way the hold is gone). BOTH outcomes settle the saga as success. `reason` is the command's cancellation reason carried through, or IDEMPOTENCY_CONFLICT on the answer to a conflicting replay (the original ledger decision stands).

**Integration topic only** (no analytics variant). Kafka key and CloudEvents `subject` are the transfer_line_id, mirroring TransferStockAllocationRejected — the release settles the line, so the line is the aggregate the fact is about.
