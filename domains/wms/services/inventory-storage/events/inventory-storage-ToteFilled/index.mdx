## Description
Loose eaches were stowed into a tote at its current bin (`POST /totes/{lpn}/fills`, ADR 0054). `ItemStowed`, `LocationRecorded` and `BinStockLevelChanged` accompany it as for any stow; it books no extra quantity.
> **No consumer by design:** pending: tote fact (ADR 0054); no consumer declared in any fleet spec yet
