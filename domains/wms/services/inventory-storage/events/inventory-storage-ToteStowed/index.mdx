## Description
A tote and all its contents now have `bin` as their logical location (`POST /totes/{lpn}/stowages`, ADR 0054). `from_bin` is omitted on the first placement. Moves inside the AS/RS never produce it (WCS seam decision 2). `BinStockLevelChanged` is raised for both bins.
> **No consumer by design:** pending: tote fact (ADR 0054); no consumer declared in any fleet spec yet
