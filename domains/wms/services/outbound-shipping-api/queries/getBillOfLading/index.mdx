## Overview
The issued document of a confirmed load: carrier, SCAC, trailer,
seal, package count and total weight. `ISSUED` or `VOIDED`.





## GET `(/bills-of-lading/{bolNumber})`

### Parameters
- **bolNumber** (path) (required): The bill of lading number.
