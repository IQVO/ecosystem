## Description
`type` =
`com.warehouse.wms.outbound-shipping.outboundload.LoadingStarted`;
subject and key = the load id. Requires a door AND a docked trailer
(`ErrTrailerNotDocked`). yard-management starts its outbound door
work on it (wave 6.4, deferred until the yard contract merges).

> **No consumer by design:** pending: ADR 0005 names yard-management as the integration consumer, but yard-management declares no such consumer and has no handler for it on develop.
