## Description
`type` =
`com.warehouse.wms.outbound-shipping.outboundload.ShipmentConfirmed`;
subject and key = the load id. Raised on `TrailerDeparted` for a
CLOSED load (ADR 0002): the goods physically left. `orders` lists
every distinct `source_order_id` of the shipped packages (ADR 0003);
a package manifested without one is excluded. order-management marks
orders Shipped/PartiallyShipped (D7) and network-fulfillment joins
`localOrderId` (ADR 0004).
