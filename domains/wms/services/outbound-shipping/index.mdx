## Purpose
Gets every manifested package onto the right load before its cutoff, proves what left on which trailer, and tells the fleet that an order has actually shipped: staging, the outbound load, ship-confirm on the trailer's physical departure, and the bill of lading.

Every message is a CloudEvents 1.0 structured-mode JSON document (`application/cloudevents+json`).
`<ctx>.events` topics carry the integration contract; `<ctx>.analytics` topics carry projector input for the
context's own reports and are never consumed by another context.

## Contract


Event contract for the **outbound-shipping** bounded context (WMS
subdomain): staging, the outbound load, ship-confirm on the trailer's
physical departure, and the bill of lading (ADR 0001, ADR 0002, ADR
0005).

**Published.** Three aggregates raise thirteen events, published to
`warehouse.outbound-shipping.events` through a **transactional outbox**:
the aggregate row and its already-encoded events are written in ONE
database transaction and a relay drains them to Kafka (at-least-once; the
CloudEvents `id` is stored with the outbox row, so a retry republishes
the same `id`). Every payload carries the aggregate `version` AFTER the
change, so consumers can reject out-of-order deliveries.

**Analytics.** Every published event is ALSO written, in the same
transaction and under the same `id`, to
`warehouse.outbound-shipping.analytics` with
`dataschema urn:warehouse:outbound-shipping:analytics:<EventName>:v1`.
That stream is not an integration contract: only this service's own
projector consumes it. Other contexts keep reading
`warehouse.outbound-shipping.events`.

**Consumed (ADR 0003, ADR 0005).** `PackageManifested` and
`PackageDiverted` from fulfillment-execution; `CPTScheduleChanged` from
process-path-management; `ZoneRegistered` and `LocationSlotRegistered`
from facility-layout; `DockAppointmentBooked` from dock-scheduling
(pinned from its `origin/feature/service` until that contract merges);
`TrailerDocked`, `DoorWorkCompleted` and `TrailerDeparted` from
yard-management (pinned to its merged contract,
`contracts/yard-management.asyncapi.yaml`, ADR 0010 there). Each feeds a durable local copy. The consumed
messages below are the producers' own contracts, decoded independently
here and never imported.

**Envelope (mandatory).** CloudEvents 1.0, structured content mode, Kafka
header `content-type: application/cloudevents+json; charset=UTF-8`.
`type` = `com.warehouse.wms.outbound-shipping.<entity>.<EventName>`
where `<entity>` is the raising aggregate lowercase with no separators:
`outboundload`, `shippingpackage`, `billoflading`. Kafka key = aggregate
id. Dispatch on the full `type`, ignore unknown types, dedupe on `id`.
