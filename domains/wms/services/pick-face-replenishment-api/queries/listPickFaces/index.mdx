## Overview
One entry per forward pick face of the site (a forward bin assigned to a
SKU by the site's latest approved slot plan), ordered by forward bin.
`available = onHand - reserved`. `status` is `BELOW_MIN` when the
projected level (available plus the open request, if any) is at or below
the policy minimum, `OK` otherwise, `NO_POLICY` when no policy is
declared, and `NO_SOURCE` when the level is low but nothing can be moved:
no reserve bin holds usable stock, or less than one `multiple` fits.
Read from the event-fed local copies (ADR 0003).





## GET `(/pick-faces)`

### Parameters
- **siteId** (query) (required): The site.
- **status** (query): Only pick faces with this status.
- **limit** (query): Page size, 1 to 500 (default 100).
- **cursor** (query): The `nextCursor` value from the previous page.
