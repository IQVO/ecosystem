## Description
`type` = `com.warehouse.wms.pick-face-replenishment.replenishment.ReplenishmentCancelled`;
subject and key = the request id. `reason` is `OPERATOR`,
`ASSIGNMENT_CHANGED` (a newer approved slot plan no longer assigns the SKU
to the forward bin) or `POLICY_REMOVED`. Consumers cancel work they have
not yet released.


> **No consumer by design:** pending: wes-work-planning consumer merged (K44); its asyncapi must declare the consumed channel
