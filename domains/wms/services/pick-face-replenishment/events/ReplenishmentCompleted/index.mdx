## Description
`type` = `com.warehouse.wms.pick-face-replenishment.replenishment.ReplenishmentCompleted`;
subject and key = the request id. `quantity` is what inventory-storage's
`StockRelocated` moved, which can be less than the requested quantity.


> **No consumer by design:** ledger mirror of inventory-storage's StockRelocated; consumed on demand
