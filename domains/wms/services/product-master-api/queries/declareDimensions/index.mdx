## Overview
Sets or replaces the declared (vendor or steward supplied) unit
dimensions in whole millimetres and weight in whole grams (ADR 0002).
Each dimension is 1..20000 mm and the weight 1..2000000 g. Returns the
whole physical profile, including the effective values and the
declared-versus-measured discrepancy flag. Identical values return 200
and change nothing.
