## Overview
Declares the pack level `level` of a registered product with its GTIN
(ADR 0011). `EACH` has no child and exactly one unit per pack; every
other level names `childGtin` (an ACTIVE lower level of this product)
and `qtyOfChild` (2 to 1000000). `unitsPerPack`, the direct factor to
eaches, is derived from the chain when omitted and must equal it when
given. `dims` are the declared dimensions of ONE pack (ADR 0002 bounds);
a pack must not weigh less than its contents when both weights are
known.

Repeating the identical declaration returns 200 and changes nothing.
`isOrderable`, `isDespatchUnit` and `dims` of an active level can be
changed in place by declaring the same `gtin` again. The content
quantity (`childGtin`, `qtyOfChild`, `unitsPerPack`) of an active level
can NOT change: that is 409 `pack-quantity-immutable`. A new quantity
is declared with a NEW `gtin` at the same level, which end-dates the
old level (reason `superseded`, kept and still resolvable) in the same
transaction; the levels above it must be declared again by the caller.
Returns 201 when the product had no level there, else 200.





## PUT `(/products/{sku}/pack-levels/{level})`

### Parameters
- **sku** (path) (required): The SKU (1..64 characters, no whitespace, control characters or `/`).
- **level** (path) (required): The packaging level, upper case.
