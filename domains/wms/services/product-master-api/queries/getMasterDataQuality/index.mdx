## Overview
Is the product master filling in? `days[]` has one row per UTC calendar
day intersecting `[from, to)` (zeros included); a day's counts cover
the part of the day inside the range. `registered`, `classified`,
`dimensions_declared` and `measured` count the products whose FIRST
such event fell in that window, so a reclassification or a
re-measurement never counts twice and the rows add up to coverage
growth. `open_discrepancies` counts the products whose latest physical
profile at the end of the window (the day's end, or `to` for a partial
last day) has measured dimensions disagreeing with the declared ones.

`coverage` is the CURRENT state of every product the projection knows,
independent of the range: ratios are over `products` (distinct SKUs
seen in any event), except `discrepancy_ratio`, which is over
`measured`; a ratio over zero is 0. The data is as fresh as
`GET /reports/freshness` says.





## GET `(/reports/master-data-quality)`

### Parameters
- **from** (query): RFC 3339 instant, inclusive. Defaults to `to` minus 30 days.
- **to** (query): RFC 3339 instant, exclusive. Defaults to now. At most 366 days after `from`.
