## Overview
Every pack level the product ever declared, retired ones included,
lowest level first and, within a level, oldest first. A product
without pack levels answers an empty `items` array.





## GET `(/products/{sku}/pack-levels)`

### Parameters
- **sku** (path) (required): The SKU (1..64 characters, no whitespace, control characters or `/`).
