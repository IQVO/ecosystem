## Overview
Lists registered products in ascending SKU order, a page at a time.
`cursor` is the opaque `nextCursor` of the previous page; a response
without `nextCursor` is the last page. Optional filters narrow the
list to products carrying a handling tag, or to products with or
without a classification.





## GET `(/products)`

### Parameters
- **limit** (query): Page size, 1 to 500 (default 100).
- **cursor** (query): The `nextCursor` value from the previous page.
- **handlingTag** (query): Only products whose classification carries this tag.
- **classified** (query): `true` = only classified products, `false` = only unclassified ones.
