## Overview
Records the latest measured dimensions and weight of ONE pack of the
ACTIVE level `level` (a cubiscan reading or a manual measurement; the
declared-versus-measured split of ADR 0002, per level). A measurement
older than the current one is 409 `stale-measurement`; `measuredAt` in
the future is 400; a measured weight below the weight of the contents
is 409 `pack-dimensions-below-contents`. Repeating the current
measurement changes nothing. Raises `ProductPackLevelMeasured`.





## PUT `(/products/{sku}/pack-levels/{level}/measured)`

### Parameters
- **sku** (path) (required): The SKU (1..64 characters, no whitespace, control characters or `/`).
- **level** (path) (required): The packaging level, upper case.
