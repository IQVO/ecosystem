## Overview
Answers which SKU and packaging level a GTIN identifies (ADR 0011). The
path GTIN is normalised to 14 digits first (8, 12, 13 and 14 digit
spellings of one number resolve identically). A retired GTIN still
resolves, with `active: false` and its `effectiveTo`, so an in-flight
ASN that names it can be read. For people, tooling and backfills: no
service calls this on a hot path, each consumer keeps its own
event-fed projection.





## GET `(/gtins/{gtin})`

### Parameters
- **gtin** (path) (required): A GTIN of 8, 12, 13 or 14 digits with a valid mod-10 check digit; normalised to 14 digits before the lookup.
