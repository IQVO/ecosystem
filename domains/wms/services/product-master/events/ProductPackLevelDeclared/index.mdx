## Description
`type` = `com.warehouse.wms.product-master.product.ProductPackLevelDeclared`;
subject and key = the SKU. Carries the FULL level after the change
(ADR 0011). `child_gtin` and `qty_of_child` are absent for EACH;
`units_per_pack` is 1 for EACH. `dims` are the DECLARED dimensions
(omitted when none); measurements arrive as `ProductPackLevelMeasured`.
Only `dims`, `is_orderable` and `is_despatch_unit` can change for a
GTIN already announced (the event is then re-sent in full); a new
content quantity is a new `gtin`.
