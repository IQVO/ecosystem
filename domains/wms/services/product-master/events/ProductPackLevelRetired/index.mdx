## Description
`type` = `com.warehouse.wms.product-master.product.ProductPackLevelRetired`;
subject and key = the SKU. `reason` is `superseded` when a new GTIN was
declared at the same level (the matching `ProductPackLevelDeclared`
follows with the next `version`), else the reason given to the retire
command. The GTIN is never reused. The levels above it are NOT retired
by this event: re-declare them.
