## Overview
How do the site's slot-plan reviews flow? `days[]` has one row per UTC
calendar day intersecting `[from, to)` (zeros included); a day's counts
cover the part of the day inside the range. `plans_generated`,
`plans_approved` and `plans_rejected` count the events whose `time`
fell in the window; `supersessions` counts the plans a later approval
superseded in the window (a supersession is stamped on the plan that
LOST the forward-slot map, not on the approving one).
`approval_latency` is the generated-to-approved time of the plans
approved in the window whose generation the projection also saw; a
plan decided without a seen generation counts in its decision counter
but has no latency. `p50_seconds` / `p95_seconds` are
linear-interpolated percentiles and are null when `count` is 0.
`siteId` narrows every counter to one site (omitted = every site);
`granularity` is `day` (the only bucket; the default). The data is as
fresh as `GET /reports/freshness` says.





## GET `(/reports/slot-plan-funnel)`

### Parameters
- **from** (query): RFC 3339 instant, inclusive. Defaults to `to` minus 30 days.
- **to** (query): RFC 3339 instant, exclusive. Defaults to now. At most 366 days after `from`.
- **siteId** (query): Narrows every counter to one site. Omitted = every site.
- **granularity** (query): The time-bucket resolution; only `day` exists (the default).
