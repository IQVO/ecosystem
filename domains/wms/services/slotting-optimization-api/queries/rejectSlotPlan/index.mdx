## Overview
Moves a `Draft` plan to `Rejected` and publishes `SlotPlanRejected`. The
optional `reason` is at most 500 characters. Rejecting a plan that is not
a `Draft` is a 409 `plan-not-draft`.





## POST `(/slot-plans/{planId}/reject)`

### Parameters
- **planId** (path) (required): The plan id (`plan-` followed by a unique suffix, at most 64 characters).
