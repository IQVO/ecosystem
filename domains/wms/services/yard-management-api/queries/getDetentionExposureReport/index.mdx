## Overview
Among the trailers that DEPARTED in the window, those whose dwell exceeded `thresholdMinutes` (default 120, the two hour norm), per trailer kind. This is exposure, a proxy: no standard definition of detention exists and charging is out of scope. A dwell equal to the threshold does not exceed it. Until the analytics read side is wired the report has no rows. `from` and `to` are required RFC 3339 instants (to after from, at most 92 days). Definitions: ADR 0011.




## GET `(/reports/detention-exposure)`

### Parameters
- **from** (query) (required): Start of the report window (RFC 3339).
- **to** (query) (required): End of the report window (RFC 3339), after `from`, at most 92 days later.
- **siteId** (query): Only this site.
- **thresholdMinutes** (query): A departed trailer whose dwell exceeds this many minutes counts as detention exposure. Defaults to 120 (the two hour norm). Anything else than an integer from 1 to 10080 is a 400 `invalid-threshold-minutes`.
