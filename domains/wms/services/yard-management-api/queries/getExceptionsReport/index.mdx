## Overview
Per site, for the window: visits checked in and rejected (rejectionRate = rejected / (checkedIn + rejected)), seal exceptions (sealExceptionRate = per check-in; it counts the exceptions raised at the gate of rejected visits too, so it is not capped at 1), trailer holds placed by reason, yard checks closed and the discrepancies they found. Rates are null when their denominator is zero. Until the analytics read side is wired the report has no rows. `from` and `to` are required RFC 3339 instants (to after from, at most 92 days). Definitions: ADR 0011.




## GET `(/reports/exceptions)`

### Parameters
- **from** (query) (required): Start of the report window (RFC 3339).
- **to** (query) (required): End of the report window (RFC 3339), after `from`, at most 92 days later.
- **siteId** (query): Only this site.
