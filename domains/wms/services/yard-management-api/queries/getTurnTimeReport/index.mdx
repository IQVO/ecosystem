## Overview
Minutes from VisitCheckedIn.arrived_at to VisitCheckedOut.departed_at (the event's on_site_minutes) per visit purpose, over the visits that checked OUT in the window, with the average, the median (p50), the 90th percentile (p90) and the maximum. Percentiles are reported because an average hides the long tail. A visit whose check-in the projection never saw has no purpose and is left out. Until the analytics read side is wired the report has no rows. `from` and `to` are required RFC 3339 instants (to after from, at most 92 days). Definitions: ADR 0011.




## GET `(/reports/turn-time)`

### Parameters
- **from** (query) (required): Start of the report window (RFC 3339).
- **to** (query) (required): End of the report window (RFC 3339), after `from`, at most 92 days later.
- **siteId** (query): Only this site.
