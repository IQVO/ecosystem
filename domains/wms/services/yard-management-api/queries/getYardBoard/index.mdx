## Overview
The live spot and door map of one site: every active Yard and Dock slot with the trailer on it, the trailers in motion and the open moves. A site with no known locations answers 404.




## GET `(/sites/{siteId}/yard-board)`

### Parameters
- **siteId** (path) (required): The site code.
