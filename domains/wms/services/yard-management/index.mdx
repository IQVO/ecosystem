## Purpose
Owns everything physically on site outside the four walls: who passed the gate (visits), which trailer is where (spot, door or an open move), its load, seal and hold state, the moves hostlers and visiting drivers make, door work at the door, and yard checks. The source of dwell, door occupancy and yard truck productivity facts.

## Contract


Event catalogue of the **yard-management** bounded context (WMS subdomain): everything physically on site outside the four walls - visits through the gate, which trailer is where, hostler and visiting-driver moves, door work and yard checks (ADR 0001, 0002).

**Published.** Four aggregates (YardVisit, Trailer, YardMove, YardCheck) raise 19 event types through a transactional outbox: the aggregate row and its events are written in ONE database transaction and a relay drains them to Kafka (at-least-once; the CloudEvents `id` is stored with the outbox row). Nine types are integration events on `warehouse.yard-management.events` (VisitCheckedIn, VisitRejected, TrailerDocked, TrailerUndocked, SealExceptionRaised, TrailerDwellExceeded, YardCheckClosed, DoorWorkCompleted, TrailerDeparted) and are mirrored on `warehouse.yard-management.analytics` under the same id; the other ten are analytics-only and go ONLY to the analytics topic. The key of every message is its aggregate root id (`visit_id`, `trailer_id`, `move_id`, `yard_check_id`).

**Consumed (ADR 0003).** dock-scheduling (appointments and door closures), facility-layout (locations and zones) and inbound-receiving (ASNs and receipts) feed event-fed local copies; no sibling is ever called live. A message that is not a valid CloudEvents 1.0 event is skipped; a valid event of an unlisted `type` is ignored; a payload that can never be applied is dead-lettered to `<topic>.dlq`; any other handler error is retried and dead-lettered after 5 attempts. Consumer groups come from `DOCK_SCHEDULING_CONSUMER_GROUP`, `FACILITY_CONSUMER_GROUP`, `INBOUND_RECEIVING_CONSUMER_GROUP` and `ANALYTICS_CONSUMER_GROUP`.

**Envelope (mandatory).** CloudEvents 1.0, structured content mode, Kafka header `content-type: application/cloudevents+json; charset=UTF-8`. `specversion`, `id`, `source` (`/warehouse/yard-management`), `type`, `subject`, `time`, `datacontenttype` (`application/json`) and `dataschema` (`urn:warehouse:yard-management:<events|analytics>:<EventName>:v1`) are all required. Type = `com.warehouse.wms.yard-management.<entity>.<EventName>`. `data` is snake_case, times are RFC 3339 UTC, `version` is the aggregate version. A breaking payload change is a new `.v2` type, never a mutation.
